## Root cause and remediation

- Equal allocation used independent half-up rounding, which can create a cent when the shared base has an odd number of cents. The fixed path converts the already-rounded base to integer cents, gives the first stable-sorted owner the floor half, and gives the second owner the exact remainder. Owner sums therefore equal, and never exceed, the authoritative shared base.
- Build computed its aggregate monthly routable cap from aggregate annual room rather than the sum of destination-level monthly routes. The fixed path computes the cap from the same per-destination rounded monthly amounts used for actual routing. The `> 0.01` escape hatch is removed; any positive post-routing remainder now violates the invariant.
- No spouse/partner discriminator, tax-filing inference, legal limit, tax treatment, allocation priority, or other financial policy was changed.
- The optional stale-ledger metadata and fixture-helper reuse findings were not changed because they are non-blocking and require broader component-semantics/test-migration decisions than the two demonstrated defects.
