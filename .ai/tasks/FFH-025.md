## Objective
Close the one remaining HIGH FFH-012 correctness defect independently reproduced by both post-FFH-023 auditors: unknown/missing legal-spouse authority is not treated as material when unresolved counterparty HSA eligibility/coverage can change the owner's legally supportable HSA amount.

This is a narrow implementation/test remediation. FFH-D005 and accepted FFH-022 authority are sufficient; there is no open policy-authority question.

## Exact dual-audit reproduction
Target HSA tax year 2026. Candidate pair A/B:
- A is confirmed eligible, under 55, no Medicare, full-year `self_only`, YTD 0;
- B is a `spouse_partner` candidate whose target-year legal-spouse authority with A is missing/`unknown`;
- B's HSA eligibility and/or coverage remains unresolved such that family coverage is still a supported resolution.

Current defect: `spouseStatusIsMaterial` can classify A as spouse-independent and expose the full 2026 self-only amount `$4,400`.

Required behavior: if a supported resolution exists in which B is a legal spouse with relevant family coverage/eligibility and A's legal amount changes (for example equal-default shared ordinary allocation `$4,375`), A's affected HSA capacity must remain targeted `more_information_needed` rather than expose an unconditional actionable `$4,400`.

Truly spouse-independent cases must remain local/actionable. Example: where both people's relevant eligibility/coverage facts are fully known self-only and spouse vs non-spouse resolution cannot change the supported owner result, unknown legal-spouse authority must not poison unrelated capacity.

## Protected semantics
Preserve FFH-022 exactly:
- authority is canonical-pair + HSA-tax-year tri-state;
- `spouse_partner`, filing status, allocation rows, and prior-year rows never create authority;
- confirmed legal spouses use the authorized married-family rules;
- confirmed non-spouses are evaluated independently;
- unknown/missing authority blocks only where spouse status can materially change the supported HSA result;
- unrelated IRA/workplace routes remain usable when independently supported.

Do not create new policy or infer legal marriage.
