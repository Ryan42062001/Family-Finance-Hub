## Objective
Close the one remaining HIGH FFH-012 correctness defect independently reproduced by both post-FFH-023 auditors: unknown/missing legal-spouse authority was not treated as material when unresolved counterparty HSA eligibility/coverage could change the owner's legally supportable HSA amount.

This was a narrow implementation/test remediation. FFH-D005 and accepted FFH-022 authority were sufficient; no policy-authority change was required.

## Exact dual-audit reproduction
Target HSA tax year 2026. Candidate pair A/B:
- A confirmed eligible, under 55, no Medicare, full-year `self_only`, YTD 0;
- B a `spouse_partner` candidate whose target-year legal-spouse authority with A is missing/`unknown`;
- B eligibility and/or coverage unresolved such that family coverage remains a supported resolution.

Before the production change, the test-only checkpoint `c9155d85985baabe9868ccb821759cc37b0317b2` reproduced the defect in Foundation CI run `34642782520`, job `103406283318`:
- A was exposed as `available` with `annualLimit = 4400`, `contributedYtd = 0`, `remainingAnnualRoom = 4400`.
- That result was not invariant because an eligible legal-spouse resolution with relevant family coverage could instead place A under the shared ordinary family ceiling; under the default equal allocation the supported amount is `$4,375`.
- Six new regressions failed before the production edit: missing authority + eligible/coverage unknown; explicit unknown authority; unknown eligibility + family; unknown eligibility + family-possible coverage; order invariance; and unrelated IRA/workplace locality because the HSA item remained incorrectly actionable.

Required behavior was therefore targeted `more_information_needed` whenever supported spouse vs non-spouse resolutions can change the owner result, without broadly poisoning truly spouse-independent cases.

## Implemented correction
`lib/calculations/money-priority-hsa-legal-capacity.ts` now evaluates unresolved legal-spouse authority with a bounded month-level predicate:
- both people must still be able to be HSA-eligible (`eligibility !== ineligible` for each); and
- family coverage must either be known or still possible for either person (`family` or `unknown`).

Only when both conditions hold is unresolved authority treated as material. This closes the compound uncertainty gap while preserving locality for fully known all-self-only facts and known-ineligible counterparties.

No legal-marriage inference was added. `spouse_partner`, filing status, allocation rows, and prior-year data remain non-authoritative.

## Protected semantics preserved
- canonical pair + HSA-tax-year tri-state authority;
- `confirmed_legal_spouses` alone authorizes married-family sharing;
- `confirmed_not_legal_spouses` remains independent;
- missing/unknown authority blocks only where spouse status can materially change supported HSA capacity;
- all-self-only spouse-independent cases remain actionable;
- target-year isolation and canonical/order invariance remain intact;
- unrelated IRA/workplace opportunities remain usable;
- FFH-023 Finding B integer-cent allocation remains closed;
- FFH-023 Finding C exact Build routing/reconciliation remains closed.
