## Manager verification of Finding D
Manager independently verified the blocker against the frozen production code and persistence contract.

At `ffde8440...`, `money-priority-hsa-legal-capacity.ts` builds `candidatePairIds` only when the active nondependent `self`/`spouse_partner` candidate count is exactly two. With three or more candidates, pair identity becomes empty and pair/year legal-spouse authority is not selected.

The `household_people` persistence contract permits multiple active `self`/`spouse_partner` rows and has no maximum-two or unique pair-cardinality constraint. The state is therefore reachable rather than a test-only impossible snapshot.

A three-candidate household can therefore ignore an otherwise valid target-year A/B authority row and allow A/B to fall through to independent family HSA evaluation. The fresh technical audit demonstrated a material overstatement path from one lawful `$8,750` shared family ordinary pool to two independent `$8,750` family limits (`$17,500`).
