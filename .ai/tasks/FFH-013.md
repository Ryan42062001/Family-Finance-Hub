## Manager finding FFH-013-M01 — recurring equal-fulfillment cent reconciliation
Severity: HIGH
Status: OPEN / BLOCKING

The submitted annual legal-capacity ledger is bounded correctly, but Build's routable-capacity prepass and its actual equal-fulfillment routing use different allocation/rounding paths.

Concrete reachable example from the submitted code:
- shared MFJ IRA compensation remaining: `$10,000.01`;
- each spouse owner-conditional remaining room: `$7,500.00`;
- the routable-capacity prepass consumes destinations sequentially and derives `$625.00 + $208.33 = $833.33` monthly capacity;
- Build therefore authorizes `$833.33` monthly retirement routing;
- the actual tied-spouse router requests `roundMoney($833.33 * 12) = $9,999.96` annually and equal-fulfillment splits `$4,999.98 / $4,999.98`;
- each account route rounds to `$416.67` monthly;
- account-level monthly routes therefore sum to `$833.34`, one cent above the Build retirement request;
- `retirementToRoute` clamps to zero after subtraction, so the mismatch is not rejected.

Annual statutory capacity is not exceeded in this example, but the authoritative Build request and account-level recurring routes do not reconcile exactly. This violates the established Phase-5 routing/accounting invariant and FFH-013 exact-cent acceptance requirement.

Remediation must use one consistent deterministic tie-routing/reconciliation method for both routable-capacity planning and actual account routing, and must add a direct Build-level regression proving exact monthly reconciliation at one-cent shared-pool boundaries. Do not weaken the routing invariant or hide the mismatch with a tolerance/epsilon.
