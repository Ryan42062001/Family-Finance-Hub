## Audit findings to close

### FFH-013-A01 — HIGH — annual tied routing is not shared-group-capped before equal fulfillment
Source: Technical T1.

Existing Cash/Windfall annual tied-spouse routing can plan against summed owner-conditional room before the shared MFJ group clips consumption. In the audited example `$7,500 + $7,500` conditional owner room with `$10,000.01` shared room and `$15,000` tied demand, stable account identity can determine a material `$7,500 / $2,500.01` split. Remediation must cap the tied amount to the common shared group before owner shares are computed.

### FFH-013-A02 — HIGH — active scheduled/current-plan IRA contributions are not reserved
Source: Technical T2.

The current ledger correctly keeps schedules separate from YTD, but it does not reserve active `monthlyEmployeeContribution` IRA amounts before Existing Cash/Secure/Build/Windfall consume planning capacity. Current-plan reservations must reduce cloned planning capacity exactly once without becoming YTD.

### FFH-013-A03 — HIGH — YTD can make shared compensation material after group creation was skipped
Source: Technical T3.

Shared MFJ group creation/warnings are gated by pre-YTD scarcity. Example: compensation `$10,000 / $5,000`, under-50 limits `$7,500 / $7,500`, YTD `$8,000 / $0`. The shared constraint must limit the second spouse to `$7,000`, not `$7,500`, and the owner excess must be surfaced.

### FFH-013-A04 — HIGH — owner-only supported excess does not fail closed for the affected shared group
Source: Policy P01.

Example: compensation `$4,000 / $2,000`, YTD `$5,000 / $0`. The frozen behavior leaves `$1,000` additional room for the other spouse. Accepted FFH-009/FFH-D006 policy requires zero new room for the affected shared group once authoritative YTD already violates an applicable individual or joint feasible-set ceiling, plus warning and no invented correction mechanics.

### FFH-013-A05 — MEDIUM — conditional maxima explanation can look independently additive
Source: Policy P02.

Household-facing IRA option/explanation output may describe each spouse as having `$X of known contribution room` without surfacing that both conditional maxima draw from one shared MFJ compensation pool. Concrete routing must remain safe and the explanation must communicate the conditional/shared nature without changing Roth/deductibility semantics.
