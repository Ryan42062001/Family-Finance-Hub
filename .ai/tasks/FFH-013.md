## Blocking finding FFH-013-P03 — HIGH

### Active IRA monthly schedule reservation uses the wrong authority/time horizon

A02's architecture is correct that scheduled/current-plan contributions are planning reservations distinct from actual YTD, but the reservation amount is currently derived from an unsupported annualization:

`max(0, monthlyEmployeeContribution * 12 - aggregate IRA YTD)`

Repository semantics treat `monthly_employee_contribution` as the active current monthly pace, while current-year retirement logic separately reasons over the remaining contribution period. The monthly field is not itself an annual target.

Blocking adversary around September 2026:
- compensation `$10,000/$0`;
- actual IRA YTD `$7,000/$0`;
- A active IRA schedule `$500/month`;
- B schedule `$0`.

Legal shared room before future schedule is `$3,000`; A owner room is `$500`. The supported future A schedule must reserve that remaining `$500` of owner/legal room, leaving at most `$2,500` for new shared recommendations. Current `12 × monthly - YTD` math can reserve `$0`, allowing later consumers to recommend the same future capacity again.

The converse late-year case may over-reserve by annualizing all 12 months instead of the supported remaining current-year schedule.

Required remediation:
- keep YTD factual and separate;
- derive future scheduled reservation from authoritative active schedule semantics for the applicable remaining current-year period, using the repository's existing date/remaining-period authority where possible;
- do not invent an annual target from the monthly field;
- if an explicit authoritative annual target is the proper source in a specific path, use it only according to its accepted contract;
- cap scheduled reservation through owner/shared legal capacity;
- reserve it exactly once before Existing Cash / Secure / Build / Windfall new allocations;
- preserve gross legal capacity separately from post-schedule planning capacity.

Required direct regressions:
- September/late-year active `$500/month` schedule with `$7,000` YTD and only `$500` owner room remaining => reserve supported `$500`, not `$0`;
- late-year low-YTD case proves no `12 × monthly` over-reservation;
- partial YTD + active schedule;
- both spouses scheduled;
- multiple IRA accounts;
- staged Existing Cash -> Secure/Build -> Windfall;
- schedule remains distinct from YTD;
- account/person order invariance.
