## Objective
Implement FFH-D006 owner-conditional IRA maxima plus one shared MFJ compensation ledger, replacing the current deterministic sorted-owner compensation allocation without changing Roth eligibility or Traditional IRA deductibility rules.

The accepted regulatory/policy problem is that scarce MFJ compensation is a shared legal constraint, not a permanent owner-ID-first statutory allocation. Core must represent owner-conditional maxima without presenting a deterministic engineering split as owner-specific legal capacity.

## Required behavior
- actual authoritative YTD consumes legal capacity exactly once;
- active schedule/current-plan allocations reserve planning capacity but are not relabeled as YTD;
- multiple IRA accounts do not multiply owner/shared room;
- owner conditional maxima remain non-additive without the shared ledger;
- shared MFJ compensation cannot be exceeded in aggregate;
- preserve owner neutrality and input-order invariance;
- use existing approved routing factors first, equal-fulfillment sharing for true financial ties, stable ID only for unavoidable final-cent remainder;
- missing material spouse compensation/owner/account/YTD facts produce targeted information-needed;
- absence of a manually entered IRA account must not prove zero spouse IRA YTD;
- possible supported excess produces zero additional room plus warning, not invented tax correction mechanics;
- preserve Roth IRA eligibility and Traditional IRA deductibility semantics;
- preserve accepted FFH-015 SIMPLE behavior and accepted FFH-012/FFH-028 HSA behavior;
- preserve the clean Type Check/lint/build baseline.

1. Scarce MFJ compensation is enforced once through a shared ledger rather than preallocated permanently by sorted owner ID.
2. Each spouse's IRA opportunity remains conditional on the other spouse's actual/YTD/planned use and does not falsely expose additive legal maxima.
3. Aggregate IRA use never exceeds supported joint compensation or applicable per-owner IRA dollar limits.
4. Multiple IRA accounts cannot multiply owner or household legal room.
5. Actual YTD, scheduled/current-plan reservations, and new recommended allocations remain semantically distinct and cannot consume capacity twice.
6. Owner/account input reordering does not change supported household legal capacity or material recommendation outcomes; unavoidable final-cent ties use stable identity only at the final remainder.
7. Missing material compensation/YTD/account facts create targeted information-needed rather than invented zero or positive room.
8. Possible supported excess clamps additional capacity to zero and surfaces a warning without fabricating correction mechanics.
9. Roth IRA eligibility and Traditional IRA deductibility behavior are unchanged.
10. FFH-015 SIMPLE, FFH-012/028 HSA, and unrelated workplace-retirement behavior remain unchanged.
11. Focused/adversarial tests cover scarce-compensation, one-spouse-low/no-compensation, asymmetric YTD, multiple IRA accounts, missing facts, excess, ordering, and exact-cent conservation.
