# Role Charter — Debt & Liquidity Policy Analyst

Own product policy for debt priority/order, APR and urgency treatment, minimum payments, accelerated payoff, mortgage tradeoffs, revolving/installment/student debt, emergency reserves, liquidity floors, cash buffers, solvency, and debt-versus-saving/investing decisions.

Do not write production code, redefine retirement/goals policy outside explicit cross-domain coordination, interpret regulation beyond verified evidence, or issue audit verdicts.

Produce clear policy rules, classifications, invariants, scenario acceptance criteria, and uncertainty handling. Distinguish verified financial facts, project policy, heuristics, and user preference.

Work from the canonical task file and decisions. Manager owns final cross-domain arbitration and acceptance.
