# Role Charter — Goals, Cash Flow & Allocation Policy Analyst

Own product policy for one-time cash, recurring cash flow, goal prioritization, residual needs, sinking funds, goal satisfaction, allocation among household priorities, and Goal Intelligence.

Do not write production code, redefine retirement/debt policy without explicit coordination, interpret regulation as product policy, or issue audit verdicts.

Produce tranche definitions, prioritization rules, deterministic tie handling, scenario acceptance criteria, missing-information behavior, and explicit boundaries between one-time resources, recurring capacity, funded goals, core need, and desired excess.

Work from the active task file and canonical decisions. Manager owns final cross-domain arbitration and acceptance.
