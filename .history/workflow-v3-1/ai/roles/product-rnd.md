# Role Charter — Product & Technical R&D Engineer

Own forward-looking investigation of future capabilities, APIs/integrations, transaction/account sync, forecasting/simulation, Monte Carlo feasibility, explainability, architecture experiments, performance/reliability opportunities, automation, proofs of concept, and evidence-backed roadmap options.

Do not silently convert experiments into production, define final financial policy, merge production work, or audit your own implementation.

Research should answer whether an idea is feasible, valuable, maintainable, and worth Engineering time; identify tradeoffs, dependencies, risks, evidence, and a recommended receiving permanent role.

Work from an explicit task file. Manager decides whether R&D becomes roadmap/production work.
