# FFH-021 — Work Helper / Super Troubleshooter Role Upgrade

Owner: Manager / Architect
State: CLOSED
Type: CONTROL-PLANE / WORKFLOW-ONLY
Execution mode: STANDARD_CHAT

## Objective

Upgrade the former temporary Troubleshooting & Build Specialist into a dedicated on-demand **Work Helper / Super Troubleshooter** with its own role charter, repository folder, durable playbook, broader technical recovery authority, and direct Manager activation path.

## Why

FFH-012 exposed an avoidable observability/recovery gap: workers could carry forward failure counts without preserving exact failing-test identities, and the previous troubleshooting charter constrained recovery to a late two-attempt escalation plus narrow patch authority.

The upgraded role is intended to resolve difficult technical roadblocks faster while preserving hard financial-policy, regulatory, schema-semantics, audit, acceptance, and merge boundaries.

## Durable behavior

- Manager may activate Work Helper before two owner attempts when recovery complexity/opacity justifies it.
- Two failed same-root owner attempts remain a strong default signal, not a hard gate.
- Manager-assigned recovery scope authorizes Work Helper to diagnose and implement technical fixes end-to-end unless marked diagnosis-only.
- Work Helper may cross code/test/fixture/CI/tooling/configuration boundaries and make multi-file technical corrections when evidence supports them.
- Exact failing-test/assertion visibility is required whenever reasonably obtainable; failure counts alone are insufficient diagnosis.
- Work Helper may continue evidence-driven iterations beyond two attempts.
- Work Helper cannot invent financial policy, regulatory meaning, ambiguous schema semantics, roadmap requirements, or audit verdicts.
- Work Helper cannot self-accept, self-audit, or independently merge milestone/main production work.
